## 🎨 What Is OpenManga?

OpenManga is a free, self-hosted program that turns your written stories into beautiful comics, webtoons, or narrated videos—all powered by artificial intelligence. You write the words, and OpenManga handles the artwork, character consistency, lettering, and even voice narration. It's like having your own personal comic studio on your computer.

Whether you're a hobbyist writer, an aspiring comic creator, or someone who just wants to see their ideas come to life, OpenManga makes it easy. You bring the story, and it brings the visuals.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🖼️ AI-Powered Illustration
OpenManga uses advanced text-to-image AI to generate artwork for every scene. You describe what happens, and it creates matching illustrations—no drawing skills required.

### 🎭 Consistent Characters
One of the hardest parts of making comics is keeping characters looking the same across panels. OpenManga solves this by remembering character designs and applying them consistently throughout your story.

### 📖 Webtoon & Comic Formats
Export your creation in classic comic book style or vertical-scroll webtoon format. Both are popular, and OpenManga supports both.

### 🎙️ Narrated Videos
Turn your story into a video with voice narration. OpenManga uses local text-to-speech (TTS) technology, so you don't need to pay for expensive voice services.

### 🔒 Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)
OpenManga doesn't lock you into a subscription. You can use your own API keys from AI providers like OpenAI or others. This keeps costs low and gives you full control.

### 🏠 Self-Hosted & Private
Everything runs on your own computer. Your stories, your generated art, and your data stay with you. No cloud storage, no third-party access.

### 📦 Runs Anywhere
Whether you're on Windows, macOS, or Linux, OpenManga runs smoothly. It's built with modern technology (Bun, React, TypeScript) and packaged with Docker for easy setup.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to pay for anything?
OpenManga itself is free. However, if you choose to use external AI services (like high-quality image generation), those services may charge you. You can start with the built-in free options and upgrade later.

### Can I use OpenManga offline?
Yes, for basic comic creation. However, some advanced AI features require an internet connection. Narrated videos using local TTS work offline.

### Will my story be shared with anyone?
No. OpenManga is self-hosted, meaning everything runs on your computer. Your story and generated artwork never leave your device.

### Can I edit the artwork OpenManga creates?
Absolutely. OpenManga exports high-resolution images that you can edit in any image software. You can also regenerate individual panels if you don't like the result.

### What if something goes wrong?
If OpenManga doesn't start or encounters an error, first try restarting your computer. Then repeat the extraction and run steps. Most issues are resolved this way. If problems persist, visit the GitHub page and check the "Issues" section for solutions.
